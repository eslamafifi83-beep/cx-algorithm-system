Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This website is run by Dr Eslam Afifi, host of The CX Algorithm Podcast, from Melbourne, Australia. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Messages. When you send a message on the contact page, we keep your name, email, the topic you picked, any detail you add and your message.
  • Calls you book. When you book a call on the booking page, we keep your name, email, your message, the time you chose and your time zone.
  • Site visits. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, to arrange and hold the call you booked, and to understand which pages people find useful. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services listed below that run the site.

Google Calendar

The booking page connects to Dr Eslam Afifi's own Google Calendar, never to yours. It uses that connection to check which times are free and to create the calendar event and invitation for a call you book. Your name, email and message are included in that event so the call can go ahead. You do not need a Google account to book.

The site's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is used only to show open times and create bookings, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Messages and bookings are stored securely with Supabase. The site is hosted by Vercel. Calendar events live in Google Calendar. These providers may store data outside Australia.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and bookings for as long as they are useful for the conversation they relate to, and delete them when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Send a message through the contact page and we will act on it within 30 days.
